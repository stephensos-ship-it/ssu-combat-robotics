Our robot

Everybody builds Force Knight

One open-source design, one parts list, one shelf of spares that fit everybody's machine. When your robot does something strange, four other people have already seen it do that.

The build sheet

Weight class
1 lb antweight, plastic
Chassis
3D-printed PLA+, printed on campus
Drive
Four N20 gearmotors, four-wheel drive
Brains
Malenki-Nano receiver and ESC on one board
Battery
2S LiPo, 300 mAh
Weapon
Wedge and forks. A spinner bolts onto the same chassis later.
Ruleset
SPARC v1.6, so it's legal at real events

Why this one

It's free, it's documented down to the print settings, and people who aren't us have won with it. That combination is rarer than it sounds.

It also takes a bolt-on spinner without redesigning anything, so the club can argue about weapons later without throwing away the robots we built first.

The weight classes

150 gFairyweight. Pocket-sized, cheap, good for practice.
1 lbAntweight. What we build. Cheap enough to own, real enough to hurt.
3 lbBeetleweight. The obvious next step. Ask us again next year.
250 lbThe class you see on television. Out of scope for us.
