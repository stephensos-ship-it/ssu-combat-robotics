Sonoma State University

Sonoma State's combat robotics team

A student team that designs, builds, and fights remote-controlled combat robots. The same sport you have seen on BattleBots, at the weight classes a student team can actually run.

Learn more Sponsor the team

The sport

Two remote-controlled machines fight inside a sealed arena until one stops moving or time runs out. If both are still running, judges score the match on damage, aggression and control.

It is a design sport before it is anything else. Whatever a team got right or wrong shows up in the first thirty seconds of a match, which makes it one of the fastest engineering feedback loops available to a student.

What the club does

Members design a robot, build it, drive it, and repair it between matches. The chassis, the wiring, the weapon and the driving are all done by students.

We build to SPARC, the construction standard used across combat robotics, so what leaves this room is legal at real events rather than only legal here.
